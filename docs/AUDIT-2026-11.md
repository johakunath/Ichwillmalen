# Visual audit, November 2026

Phase 0 of the "top-quality kids' app" brief. All ten screens were captured at 1280×800 (landscape) and 800×1280 (portrait) with `node tools/audit-screens.cjs` (contact sheets land in the ignored `test-results/audit/`). This is a desktop-browser audit: it shows what looks cheap or unfinished, not how the app feels on the Tab S9.

## The main finding

The app has two picture qualities side by side. Ten Zauberwasser pictures and 21 Bunte Bilder pages are detailed ChatGPT illustrations and look good. The other 22 Zauberwasser pictures, 12 Bunte Bilder pages, all 25 stickers, the Suchspaß figures and every home card are flat geometric drawings (a circle and a triangle for a fish). The first picture a child sees in Bunte Bilder and Farbenfreunde is that fish.

Almost nothing shows what he loves: there are 2 dinosaurs, 1 excavator and no building bricks in the whole library.

## Across the app

| Problem | Where | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beige and sage pastel palette with low contrast | every screen | Reads as a calm adult stationery app; commercial kids' apps use strong, warm colours. |
| Small grey text carries meaning | home cards, activity headers, Zauberspuren toolbar | He cannot read; the text is noise for him. |
| Portrait pictures on a landscape tablet | Zauberwasser, Bilderpuzzle, Bunte Bilder | The picture uses about a third of the screen width in landscape. New pictures should be landscape. |
| No ending | every activity | Rounds stop on a small check mark or just continue. Nothing closes, parks or goes to sleep. |
| No motion | navigation | Screens cut hard from one to the next. |
| A blue steam engine with a face | `water/rainbow-train.webp`, `coloring/train.png` | Too close to a well-known TV engine. Replace with an original locomotive. |

## Screen by screen

| Screen | What looks cheap, confusing or unfinished |
| --- | --- |
| Home (Spielzimmer) | A text banner fills the top third. Six small cards with thin pastel drawings; the pictures do not show what the activity does. Three activities are hidden behind "Noch mehr entdecken". No grouping. In portrait the lower half of the screen is empty. |
| Malatelier | The layout works (canvas first). Tool icons are small and all look alike in grey-green; no pen invites him to try it. Covered in phase 2. |
| Zauberwasser | The picture sits in a narrow portrait frame in the middle of a wide empty screen. The cover is a pale wash that already gives the picture away. 22 of 32 pictures are flat shapes. |
| Bunte Bilder | Opens with the geometric fish. Thin grey frame around the page. The good ChatGPT pages have many very small areas (flowers, grass) that are hard to hit. The PNG pages are 1.1 to 1.4 MB each (24 MB in total) and can shrink to about 50 KB each as 1-bit images. |
| Klebewelten | Background is two green waves. Stickers are simple faces without bodies; the only vehicles are a car, a boat and a rocket, and there are no dinos. The tray is small for a 4-year-old's fingers. |
| Bilderpuzzle | The best-looking screen, because it uses the detailed pictures. It also shows the 22 flat pictures. It reuses every Zauberwasser picture, so the two activities show the same content. |
| Suchspaß | Eight clip-art figures in two rows on pastel hills. Nothing is hidden, so there is nothing to search for. It is not a Wimmelbild. |
| Zauberspuren | A dotted triangle on white paper. The toolbar has tiny text labels. Nothing happens that would make him want the next shape. |
| Kästchenkunst | Opens on an empty grid with no picture to copy in sight. |
| Farbenfreunde | The geometric fish again. In portrait the picture floats in a tall white card with empty bands above and below. |

## What is already good

- Canvas-first Malatelier with visible tools (the owner's earlier requirement holds).
- The puzzle mechanics, the water reveal and tap-to-fill work and are calm.
- Navigation is by picture and icon; the parent gate is in place.
- The ten detailed scenes prove the point of this phase: with good pictures the same activity looks like a different app.

## Consequences for the plan

1. Pictures first (see [ART_BIBLE.md](ART_BIBLE.md) and its content plan). New pictures are landscape 3:2.
2. Bilderpuzzle gets its own picture list instead of reusing Zauberwasser.
3. Suchspaß needs real Wimmelbild backgrounds with partly hidden sprites, which is an activity change, not just an image swap.
4. Retired pictures leave the pickers but their files stay, so saved drafts and album entries that point to them still open.
5. The home screen redesign (phase 1) should also set the colour palette and the activity headers for the whole app.
