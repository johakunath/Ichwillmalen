# The app through a four-year-old's eyes (2026-11-24)

Looked at on 1280×800 landscape screenshots (`node tools/audit-screens.cjs`), after the first picture batch and the fullscreen button. He cannot read, taps with a finger, draws with the S Pen, loves dinosaurs, vehicles and bricks. Ranked by how much each point costs him. Not device findings: nothing here was watched on the tablet.

## What works for him

- The new pictures: bold lines, dinos, diggers, a rocket. He recognises them at once in the picker.
- Bunte Bilder: twelve big colour dots, one tap fills. Nothing to explain.
- Bilderpuzzle: four big pieces, a faint picture underneath shows where they go.
- The house button is in the same place on every screen.
